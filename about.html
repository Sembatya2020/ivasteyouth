EMPOWERING YOUTH THROUGH MUSIC AND THE ARTS

Ivaste Youth Talents Organization is dedicated to empowering orphaned and underserved youth through the transformative power of music and arts education.

We believe every young person deserves an opportunity to express themselves creatively. Through music, dance and performance, we help youth discover and nurture their talents, build confidence and find a supportive community.

Ivaste is a youth-led nonprofit founded by Ivan Sembatya, who grew up in the ghettos of Uganda and found his voice through music. Today we train young people in Uganda and Kenya, and we are creating a Pan-African movement of hope through music.

COMPOSE. PERFORM. TRANSFORM.

Compose

We help young people explore their musical talents through songwriting, vocal training and instrumental skills.

Perform

Concerts, workshops and community events give young artists a platform to share what they create.

Transform

Creative expression, mentorship and practical outreach help build confidence, connection and opportunity.

GROWING STRONGER COMMUNITIES

Our outreach includes food distribution, scholastic materials, clothing and mentorship for young people and families in underserved communities.
