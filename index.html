HELPING YOUNG PEOPLE FIND THEIR VOICE

At Ivaste Youth Talents Organization, we believe every young person deserves the chance to express themselves creatively. Through music and the performing arts, we help youth discover their strengths, grow in confidence and imagine new possibilities. Our work also reaches beyond the stage, with practical support and mentorship for underserved communities.

A youth-led movement for change

Ivaste was founded by Ivan Sembatya, who grew up in the ghettos of Uganda and found his voice through music. Today we nurture talent and character together: singing, songwriting, dance and performance, alongside mentorship and life coaching.

From our local work in Uganda to training over 300 students in Malindi, Kenya, we are building a Pan-African movement of hope through music.
